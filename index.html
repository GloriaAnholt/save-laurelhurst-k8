Portland Public Schools’ Rightsizing plans would turn seven K-8 schools into K-5s and send their 6th through 8th graders to middle schools. Laurelhurst is one of them.

As Laurelhurst parents, we can’t speak for other schools’ families, so we use our own school as the worked example: we went through PPS’s own budgets, state test data, and bus records to check the case for the change. Every number below links to its source.

Which K-8s does PPS keep?

PPS says a K-8 needs at least 500 students to be sustainable. Only three K-8s are forecast to reach that in 2026-27, and both plans convert all three. Every K-8 that PPS keeps is forecast below PPS’s own minimum of 500. And once their middle graders leave, six of the seven converted K-8s would start out below PPS’s 360-student minimum for a K-5, given their current boundaries.
