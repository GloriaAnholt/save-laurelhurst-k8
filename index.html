Portland Public Schools has proposed turning Laurelhurst into a K-5 and sending its 6th through 8th graders to Mt Tabor Middle School. We are Laurelhurst parents who went through PPS’s own budgets, state test data and bus records to check the case for that change.

Every number below links to its source. Pick an issue to see the math.
