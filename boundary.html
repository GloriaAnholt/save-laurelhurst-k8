Why is Mt Tabor short of students?

In 2023, PPS redrew Mt Tabor Middle School’s attendance area, making it about 35% smaller. Three years later, PPS proposes filling the building by moving Laurelhurst’s 6th through 8th graders there.

What changed

  • Before 2023, Mt Tabor’s attendance area covered the Glencoe and Atkinson elementary areas, about 2.5 square miles.
  • In 2023, PPS’s southeast boundary changes took the Atkinson area (about 0.7 square miles) out of Mt Tabor’s area. A wedge north of Burnside between NE 60th and the high school boundary also moved from Glencoe to Vestal.
  • For 2026-27, Mt Tabor’s area is the Glencoe area alone, about 1.6 square miles.
  • Enrollment followed: about 740 students in 2018 and about 430 in 2025-26. Of today’s 430, 222 are in the Japanese immersion program, which students enter by lottery from across the district.
  • An audit PPS commissioned of that process heard from Glencoe families who were assigned to Harrison Park even though Mt Tabor was their closer neighborhood middle school.

Mt Tabor didn’t shrink on its own: PPS’s 2023 boundary redraw left Mt Tabor’s neighborhood too small to fill the school. Even if every eligible family chose Mt Tabor, it would still only be about 40% full.

What it means for Laurelhurst

  • About 29% of Laurelhurst’s attendance area is more than 1.5 miles from Mt Tabor by street, the distance at which PPS buses middle schoolers. PPS runs no general bus routes to Laurelhurst today. See the busing math →
  • Laurelhurst’s building is already 93% full, and 85% of the PPS students in its area choose it. See the neighborhood numbers →

Both Rightsizing plans, Scenarios A and B, already redraw Mt Tabor’s boundary. Before moving Laurelhurst’s middle graders, PPS should show whether rebalancing the neighborhoods around Mt Tabor could fill the school instead.

Sources and method

  • 2026-27 boundaries: PPS 2026-27 Attendance Boundary & District Properties map (48×36 in.). We traced Mt Tabor’s and Laurelhurst’s areas from it; the tracing is accurate to roughly half a block.
  • Pre-2023 boundaries: National Center for Education Statistics, School Attendance Boundary Survey (2015-16), which agrees with the older School Attendance Areas layer on PortlandMaps.
  • Areas are measured from those boundary shapes and rounded.
  • Enrollment and immersion counts: PPS enrollment data via ppsdata.info, 2018 and 2025-26. The “about 40%” figure divides the 271 PPS students living in Mt Tabor’s area (PPS capture-rate report, October 2024) by the building’s functional capacity in PPS’s 2021 facilities data.
  • Glencoe families and Harrison Park: Green and Hanson, Southeast Guiding Coalition (SEGC) Equity Audit Evaluation Report, prepared for PPS, June 10, 2026. The audit describes families’ experiences; it does not list the boundary changes or include maps.
  • Distance from Laurelhurst: street-grid distance from each part of Laurelhurst’s attendance area to Mt Tabor, compared with the 1.5-mile middle-school busing threshold in Oregon rule OAR 581-023-0040. By straight-line distance, only about 2% of the area is beyond 1.5 miles.
